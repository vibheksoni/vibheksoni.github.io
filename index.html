Introduction

Hello, I am Vibhek Soni. I got into programming by trying to understand the tools people used to attack other players on PlayStation. That curiosity led me to source code, networking tools, and eventually writing my own code in Notepad before I knew how it worked.

I learned on my first laptop: Python first, then C, C#, WinForms, sockets, TCP servers, epoll, ZMap, and client-server systems. Today I build backend and AI infrastructure, browser automation, and protocol tools that handle large streams of requests.

The pattern is the same for everything I work on: take something I do not understand, break it apart, figure out how it works, and build something with what I learned.
